Recent releases

This week →
  • 12 Nov 2026Lovable connectors: Deel and Retool

    Lovable added Deel and Retool as app and chat connectors. Deel lets apps read and update contracts, people, timesheets and time off; Retool lets apps manage your Retool organisation's members, groups and permissions and embed existing Retool apps. Add them from Connectors or ask Lovable in a project.

    Source
  • 11 Nov 2026Lovable Automations

    Automations let Lovable take care of recurring work, such as a morning check of your published sites, a weekly summary of new signups, or a Friday list of the oldest items in your backlog. Ask for one in Chats and say how often, and Lovable posts what it finds there. Available on Free, Pro and Business plans, and each run is chat usage.

    Source
  • 1 Nov 2026Lovable: download an SBOM for your project

    Lovable now lets you download a software bill of materials for your project from the dependency list in the Security view, in SPDX 2.3 or CycloneDX 1.5 format. It lists production dependencies and their known vulnerabilities, so you can hand it to a security or procurement team. Downloading is free.

    Source
  • 30 Oct 2026Lovable project and personal memory

    Lovable now remembers preferences, decisions and facts you state while building and applies them in later conversations. Project memory is shared by everyone who can edit a project; personal memory covers how you like to work across all projects. Memory settings show a plain-language summary you can correct or switch off.

    Source
  • 29 Oct 2026Lovable: import apps from Replit, Bolt, Base44, v0 or Manus, or an existing website

    You can now bring a project built elsewhere into Lovable and keep building: upload a ZIP export from Replit, Bolt, Base44, v0 or Manus, or enter the address of an existing site, including Wix, Squarespace or Webflow sites. The first import is free; later imports use standard build credits.

    Source
  • 27 Oct 2026Lovable Events & Hooks

    Business and Enterprise workspace admins can connect their own services: Events notify your HTTPS endpoint when a project is created, published, transferred or deleted, and Hooks let your endpoint allow or block an action first, such as screening chat messages for sensitive data. Also new: a Scrive connector for e-signatures and eID.

    Source
  • 20 Oct 2026Lovable can create your Supabase project

    Ask Lovable to create a Supabase project for an app with no backend: pick the organisation and region and Lovable creates and connects it, billed to your own Supabase org.

    Source
  • 15 Oct 2026Lovable: AI models and tools inside your apps

    Apps built in Lovable can now use GPT-Live voice, the latest Claude models, Gemini 4 Argon, Gemini Omni, Lyria, Perplexity, Firecrawl, Parallel and Jev without leaving Lovable.

    Source
