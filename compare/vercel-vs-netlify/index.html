Vercel vs Netlify for AI app deployment (2026)

Vercel vs Netlify for deploying AI apps: verdict, pricing checked 16 Oct 2026, who each suits, and FAQs.

The short answer

Vercel is the smoothest choice for Next.js and AI apps (AI SDK 7, AI Gateway, previews); Pro is $20/month. Netlify is simpler and cheaper to start for static sites, with a $9/month Personal plan.

Questions people ask

Do both have free plans?

Yes: Vercel Hobby ($0) and Netlify Free ($0).

What's Vercel Token Pass?

Announced 15 Oct 2026 in early access for Pro teams: $30/month for $80 of GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 credit through AI Gateway.

Can bills spike?

Both have usage-based extras (Vercel usage credit, Netlify credits). Set spend limits.
