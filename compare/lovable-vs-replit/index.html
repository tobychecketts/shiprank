Lovable vs Replit (2026): which should a non-coder build with?

Lovable vs Replit Agent compared: free plans, paid plans ($25 vs $20/month), publishing apps and who each suits. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026.

Updated 10 Nov 2026. Prices from the official pricing pages.

The short answer

Pick Lovable for the quickest path from an idea to a polished web app. Pick Replit if you want building, databases and publishing in one place, and want the cheaper paid plan (Core is $20/month against Lovable's $25). Replit's free plan is fine for a first try, but your one free published app goes offline after 30 days.

The main differences

  • Free plans. Lovable's free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month). Replit's free Starter plan gives a daily Agent allowance and one published app, which carries a "Made with Replit" badge and goes down after 30 days.
  • Paid plans. Replit Core is $20/month ($18 billed annually) and includes $20 of monthly credits and unlimited published apps. Lovable Pro is $25/month for 100 credits.
  • Connecting other services. On Replit, third-party connectors like Stripe and Google need Core. Lovable's paid credits also cover AI features built into your app.

Questions people ask

Is Replit free?

Yes, the Starter plan is free with no time limit. It includes a daily Agent allowance, monthly cloud credits and one published app that goes down after 30 days (Replit docs, checked 10 Nov 2026).

Which is cheaper, Lovable or Replit?

Replit Core is $20/month ($18 billed annually). Lovable Pro is $25/month ($21 billed annually). Heavier use costs more on both.

Can I take my app off Replit's badge?

Removing the "Made with Replit" badge needs the Core plan or higher.
