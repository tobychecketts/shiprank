Builder.io vs Figma Make (2026): which turns your designs into code?

Builder.io vs Figma Make for design-to-code: free plans, Builder.io Pro at $24 vs a $16 Figma Full seat, prototype vs production code. Checked 10 Nov 2026.

Updated 10 Nov 2026. Prices from the official pricing pages.

The short answer

Pick Figma Make if you want a working prototype from your designs without leaving Figma. It's our No. 1 design-to-code tool, and a Professional Full seat is $16/month. Pick Builder.io if the code has to land in a real codebase and use your own components. It connects to GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket and has a free plan. Builder.io is a ShipRank affiliate, which doesn't change its No. 2 spot.

The main differences

  • What comes out. Figma Make turns prompts and designs into functional prototypes and web apps inside Figma. Builder.io connects to your Git repo and design system, with a Figma plugin and a VS Code extension, so changes go into your codebase.
  • Free plans. Figma's Starter plan includes Make with up to 150 AI credits a day (500 a month), but no team libraries. Builder.io Free gives 15 credits a day (60 a month) and one design system.
  • Paid plans. A Figma Professional Full seat is $16/month with 3,000 AI credits. Dev seats only let you try Make in drafts. Builder.io Pro is $24/user/month with 500 credits and up to 3 design systems. Extra credits are $25 per 500.

Questions people ask

Is Figma Make free?

Yes, with limits. It's on Figma's free Starter plan, which has 150 AI credits a day and up to 500 a month. Starter users must also publish to the Figma Community to publish to the public web (Figma Help Center, checked 10 Nov 2026).

Is Builder.io free?

Yes. Builder.io's free plan gives 15 credits a day (60 a month max) and includes Git connections, the Figma plugin and the VS Code extension.
