Supabase vs Firebase for vibe-coded apps (2026)

Supabase vs Firebase for AI-built apps: verdict, free tiers and pricing checked 16 Oct 2026, who each is best for, and FAQs.

The short answer

For vibe-coded web apps, Supabase is the default: Postgres, auth and storage, with built-in support in tools like Lovable and Bolt, plus new agent-friendly code-first features. Firebase is a solid alternative if you're in the Google ecosystem; its Spark plan needs no payment method.

Questions people ask

Which is free?

Both: Supabase has a $0 Free plan (free projects pause when idle); Firebase's Spark plan is no-cost with no payment method needed.

What do paid plans cost?

Supabase Pro starts at $25/month. Firebase Blaze is pay-as-you-go; eligible users may get $300 free credit. Per-product Firebase rates weren't checked.

What's new for agents?

At Supabase Select (2 Oct 2026) Supabase announced code-first schema/config, Docker-free local Supabase (alpha) and MCP servers for your app.
