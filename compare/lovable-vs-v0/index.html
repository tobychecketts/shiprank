Lovable vs v0 (2026): full-stack app builder or React UI generator?

Lovable vs v0 by Vercel: what each builds, free limits, paid plans ($25 vs $30/month) and who should use which. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026.

Updated 10 Nov 2026. Prices from the official pricing pages.

The short answer

Pick Lovable if you want a whole working app without touching code. Pick v0 if you're happy with React/Next.js and want polished UI that syncs to GitHub and deploys to Vercel. For most non-coders, Lovable is the easier start, and it's also cheaper ($25/month against $30/user/month).

The main differences

  • What you get. Lovable builds full web apps by chat, and runs them for you on its Cloud. v0 lists deploying to Vercel, syncing with GitHub and a visual Design Mode on every plan, including Free.
  • Free limits. Lovable's free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month). v0's gives 7 messages a day.
  • Paid plans. Lovable Pro is $25/month for 100 credits. v0 Plus is $30/user/month, which includes $30 of monthly credits plus $2 of free credits each day you log in.

Questions people ask

Is v0 free?

Yes. v0's free plan includes 7 messages a day, deploys to Vercel, Design Mode and GitHub sync (checked 10 Nov 2026).

How much is Lovable compared with v0?

Lovable Pro is $25/month for 100 credits ($21/month billed annually). v0 Plus is $30 per user per month with $30 of included monthly credits.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not for Lovable. v0 produces React/Next.js code, so it helps if you can read it.
