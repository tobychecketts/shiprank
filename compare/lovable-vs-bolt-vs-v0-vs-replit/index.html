Lovable vs Bolt.new vs v0 vs Replit (2026): best AI app builder for vibe coders

Lovable, Bolt.new, v0 and Replit compared: quick verdict, verified pricing (16 Oct 2026), who each is best for, and FAQs.

The short answer

Lovable is the fastest route from a prompt to a full-stack app if you don't code. v0 produces the best-looking React/Next.js UI. Bolt.new is great for quick in-browser full-stack prototypes. Replit is the all-in-one choice when you want building and hosting in one place.

Who should pick what

Lovable
Non-coders shipping a full-stack web app, especially with Supabase.
Bolt.new
Builders who want to prototype in the browser with nothing to install.
v0
Front-end builders on React/Next.js who will deploy on Vercel.
Replit
People who want an agent plus hosting in one product and don't mind usage-based costs.

Questions people ask

Which has the most generous free tier?

Hard to compare directly: Lovable gives 5 daily build credits (up to 30/month), v0 gives 7 messages/day, Replit has a Free Mode, Bolt has a free plan (limits not compared here).

How much is Lovable Pro?

Unverified: the paid price wasn't shown on the pricing page we fetched on 16 Oct 2026. Check lovable.dev/pricing.

Do I own the code?

Lovable's pricing FAQ says you own the code and apps you build. We haven't verified the equivalent terms for the others.
