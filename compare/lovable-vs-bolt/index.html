Lovable vs Bolt.new (2026): which AI app builder should you use?

Lovable vs Bolt.new compared for people who don't code: free plans, the $25 paid plans, hosting and domains, and which one we'd pick. Prices checked 10 Nov 2026.

Updated 10 Nov 2026. Prices from the official pricing pages.

The short answer

Pick Lovable if you don't code and want the shortest route from a description to a working full-stack web app. It's our No. 1 app builder. Pick Bolt.new if you want to tinker in the browser on a bigger free allowance, with hosting included even on the free plan. Both start at $25/month when you go paid.

The main differences

  • How usage is counted. Lovable uses credits: a small change like "make the button gray" costs about 0.5 credits, and a landing page with generated images about 1.7, according to its pricing page. Bolt.new counts tokens: 1M a month on the free plan (300K a day), and Pro starts at 10M a month with no daily limit.
  • Hosting and domains. Bolt.new includes website hosting and unlimited databases on every plan. Custom domains and removing the Bolt branding need Pro. Lovable says hosting a small or new app is usually covered by the Cloud credits included with every plan.
  • Teams. Lovable charges per workspace, not per seat, so you can invite as many people as you like to share the credits. Bolt.new Teams is $30 per member a month.

Questions people ask

Is Lovable or Bolt.new cheaper?

They cost the same to start: Lovable Pro is $25/month for 100 credits ($21/month billed annually) and Bolt.new Pro is $25/month, with up to 28% off billed yearly. Checked 10 Nov 2026.

Which free plan is better?

They measure usage differently. Lovable's free plan gives 5 build credits a day (up to 30 a month). Bolt.new's gives 1M tokens a month, up to 300K a day, and puts Bolt branding on your site.

Can I use my own domain?

On Bolt.new, custom domains are part of Pro ($25/month). We haven't confirmed Lovable's custom domain terms on its own pages this time, so check its pricing page before you buy.

Do I own what I build?

Lovable's pricing FAQ says you own the code and the apps you build. We haven't verified Bolt.new's terms on this point.
